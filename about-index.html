Our mission

Dubai is one of the most dynamic cities in the world — but navigating it as an expat, investor or entrepreneur means wading through contradictory advice, outdated guides and intermediaries with their own agendas.

We built mydubaiguide.biz to change that. Our AI-powered matching engine combines deep market knowledge with live data from sources like Bayut and the Dubai Land Department, and delivers a clear, honest recommendation in minutes — not days.

We don't earn commissions from agents pushing specific properties. We don't take kickbacks from free zone operators. We surface the right answer for your situation, then connect you with a vetted specialist if you want one.

How we make money

We are transparent about this. When you request a specialist introduction through our platform, we may earn a referral fee from the specialist — property agent, business setup firm or financial advisor — if they successfully help you. This fee is paid by the specialist, not by you.

For credit card recommendations, we may earn a commission from the bank when a card is successfully applied for through our links. This does not affect which cards we recommend — our algorithm matches cards to your profile, not to our commission rate.

All recommendations are generated by AI based on your answers and our market data, not by which partner pays us the most.
