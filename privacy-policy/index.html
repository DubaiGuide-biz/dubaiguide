Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how mydubaiguide.biz, operated by Monica Ghangale ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores and shares your personal data. By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who we are

mydubaiguide.biz is a UAE-based AI-powered matching platform that helps individuals find the right property, business setup and credit card options in Dubai. The platform is operated by Monica Ghangale, based in the UAE.

For data-related queries, contact us at: hello@mydubaiguide.biz

2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information: Your name, email address and phone number, when you submit a lead form or email capture on our platform.
  • Quiz answers: Your responses to our matching quizzes, including budget, property type, business type, salary range, spending habits and preferences. This data is not directly identifiable on its own but becomes identifiable when combined with your contact information.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks and navigation behaviour, collected automatically via Google Analytics 4.
  • Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, device type and approximate location, collected automatically.

3. How we use your data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To generate your personalised AI-powered recommendation based on your quiz answers.
  • To send your match result to your email address when you request it.
  • To share your contact details and quiz answers with vetted specialist partners — property agents, business setup firms, mortgage brokers or credit card advisors — when you explicitly request a specialist introduction.
  • To notify you of relevant guides, updates and services where you have consented to receive such communications.
  • To improve our platform, recommendations and user experience through aggregated, anonymised analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing your data with third parties

This is important — please read carefully.

When you submit your details through our lead form or specialist introduction request, your name, email, phone number and quiz answers will be shared with one or more of our specialist partners relevant to your enquiry. These partners are:

  • Licensed UAE real estate agents and brokers (for property enquiries)
  • Licensed UAE business setup and PRO service firms (for business enquiries)
  • UAE-licensed mortgage brokers and financial advisors (for property and credit enquiries)

By submitting a lead form, you explicitly consent to this sharing. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing hello@mydubaiguide.biz.

We do not sell your data to data brokers, advertising networks or any party not directly related to fulfilling your enquiry.

We use the following third-party services which may process your data:

  • Resend — for email delivery
  • Vercel — for website hosting
  • Google Analytics 4 — for anonymised usage analytics
  • Anthropic — for AI-powered recommendation generation (quiz answers are sent to Anthropic's API but not stored by Anthropic beyond the immediate request)

5. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL):

  • Consent: For sharing your data with specialist partners and for sending marketing communications.
  • Legitimate interest: For improving our platform and services through aggregated analytics.
  • Contractual necessity: For delivering the service you have requested, including generating your recommendation and facilitating specialist introductions.

6. How long we keep your data

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by UAE law. Lead data is retained for a maximum of 24 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Your rights

Under UAE PDPL, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@mydubaiguide.biz. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics 4, which places cookies on your device to collect anonymised usage data. By using this website, you consent to this use. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. However, no internet transmission is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Our platform is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
